# Impasto demo: script

3 minutes at most. Sultan narrates, and Yara speaks for herself on camera. Live app on screen, cut with Yara's real photos and code-built animated graphics (type, diagrams). No generated scenes. Photos are in `demo/photos/` (kept out of git).

Format follows the Crossbeam demo (Built with Opus 4.6): hook line → who I am → the real person, in her own words → "I thought this would be easy" → simple on top → under the hood → proof → callback, then the last word goes to the real person.

Spine: one painting from idea to finished canvas, then two more she made with Impasto.

## 0. Hook (≈ 0:00–0:08), still to write

One flip line in the "Everyone thinks X. It's actually Y." pattern, as animated type on a plain background, then the Impasto wordmark. Section 7 calls it back.

## 1. Yara (≈ 0:08–0:30), locked

> *(Photo `stages` 00: the blank canvas, slow push in.)*
> "Hi, I'm Sultan. I've been building Impasto, a co-pilot for painters, to help my wife, Yara.
> About a year ago, Yara started painting, and it's become the thing she loves most."
> *(Photo `stages` 05: close-up of the ridges, slow pan.)*
> "She paints impasto: thick paint, laid on with a knife, so it stands up off the canvas. Every color is mixed by hand."

## 2. Yara, in her own words (≈ 0:30–0:42), she records

A clip of Yara talking, with a big name card ("Yara / painter"). What she says is hers. It isn't scripted for her.

## 3. "I thought this would be easy" (≈ 0:42–1:05), still to write

The Day 1 findings, over close-ups of her ridges: SAM's text prompts found nothing for "cream" or "sage"; Muse Image gave generic texture until it saw her paintings; the knife ridges get in the way of measuring color.

## 4. On top, it's simple (≈ 1:05–1:35), still to write

The live app: brief → concepts → on the wall → colors and mixing. Waiting on what Yara typed or said when she started the piece on the live site.

## 5. Under the hood (≈ 1:35–2:00), still to write

An animated diagram that builds one node per sentence: Muse Spark writes the brief → Muse Image paints the concept → SAM picks out the bands → band area becomes grams of paint and paste.

## 6. Proof: a check-in (≈ 2:00–2:25), still to write

A real check-in, recorded now, using one of her plum bowl photos (`mixes` 01, 05 or 06). The voiceover says it's a photo from while she was painting. It doesn't claim she used check-ins on this painting. The measured result is shown big on screen, labeled as saved.

## 7. Finished, and the callback (≈ 2:25–2:50), still to write

Photo `stages` 12, then the two other paintings (`others` 00, 01), both made with Impasto. Sultan calls back the hook line.

## 8. Yara's last word (≈ 2:50–3:00), she records

The film ends on Yara's voice, over the finished canvas or her on camera.

## Rules

- Yara paints for love of it. No clients or commissions.
- Saved model results are labeled as saved.
- Don't mention how long a painting takes her.
- Yara's lines are her own words, not written for her.
